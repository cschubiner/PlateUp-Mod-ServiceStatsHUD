[h1]Service Stats HUD[/h1]
See how everyone helped during your restaurant day with a compact, configurable text overlay.

[list]
[*]srv: customer order items served at delivery
[*]ord: orders taken
[*]wash: dishes washed and floor messes cleaned
[*]act: supported player interactions, including preparation and service
[*]dist: distance traveled
[*]idle and asleep: time without an action after separate configurable thresholds
[/list]

Player names come from the profiles selected at the beds, use player colors, and sort alphabetically. The list supports larger lobbies, including MMO Kitchen.

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Open Preferences, then Service Stats HUD. Toggle individual stats, change text size (30%-130%), choose a font, adjust vertical position, and configure idle/asleep thresholds. Washed and actions are hidden by default. Players are hidden until their first serve by default; choose Show Everyone to display them immediately.

Your previous-day results remain through the next preparation phase. Stats reset when the next gameplay phase starts. Returning to HQ may clear them.

[h2]Dependencies[/h2]
Requires Harmony, KitchenLib, and PreferenceSystem. MMO Kitchen is optional.
If you previously installed this mod manually, keep only one copy of Service Stats HUD to avoid duplicate loading.
